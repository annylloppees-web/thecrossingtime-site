O resultadoLe résultatThe result

O presidente José Maria Neves, de 66 anos, apoiado pelo PAICV (centro-esquerda), foi reeleito no primeiro turno da eleição presidencial de domingo, 15 de novembro. Segundo a RTP, com informação da agência Lusa, ele tinha 63,7% dos votos com 93% das mesas apuradas, de acordo com os dados oficiais. O SBT News, com 1.214 das 1.296 mesas apuradas (93,7%), registrou 63,8% para Neves e 31,6% para Joana Rosa, apoiada pelo MpD, partido do governo.

Le président José Maria Neves, 66 ans, soutenu par le PAICV (centre gauche), a été réélu dès le premier tour de l'élection présidentielle du dimanche 15 novembre. Selon la RTP, citant l'agence Lusa, il recueillait 63,7 % des voix avec 93 % des bureaux dépouillés, d'après les données officielles. SBT News, avec 1 214 des 1 296 bureaux dépouillés (93,7 %), relevait 63,8 % pour M. Neves et 31,6 % pour Joana Rosa, soutenue par le MpD, le parti au gouvernement.

President José Maria Neves, 66, backed by the centre-left PAICV, was re-elected in the first round of Sunday's presidential election on 15 November. According to Portuguese broadcaster RTP, citing the Lusa news agency, he had 63.7% of the vote with 93% of polling stations counted, based on official data. SBT News, with 1,214 of 1,296 stations counted (93.7%), recorded 63.8% for Neves and 31.6% for Joana Rosa, backed by the governing MpD party.

Joana Rosa, ex-ministra da Justiça e primeira mulher a disputar a Presidência de Cabo Verde, reconheceu a derrota às 21h locais, na sede de campanha na Praia, enquanto a contagem ainda prosseguia. "Já oportunamente apresentei ao Presidente reeleito as felicitações", disse, segundo a Lusa. Concorreram também Gilson Alves, Casimiro Pina, Antero Tavares e Loisito Sousa. O mandato é de cinco anos.

Joana Rosa, ancienne ministre de la Justice et première femme candidate à la présidence du Cap-Vert, a reconnu sa défaite à 21 h, heure locale, à son siège de campagne à Praia, alors que le dépouillement se poursuivait. « J'ai déjà présenté mes félicitations au président réélu », a-t-elle déclaré, selon Lusa. Gilson Alves, Casimiro Pina, Antero Tavares et Loisito Sousa étaient aussi candidats. Le mandat est de cinq ans.

Joana Rosa, a former justice minister and the first woman to run for the presidency of Cape Verde, conceded at 9pm local time at her campaign headquarters in Praia while counting was still under way. "I have already congratulated the re-elected President," she said, according to Lusa. Gilson Alves, Casimiro Pina, Antero Tavares and Loisito Sousa also ran. The term is five years.

Abstenção recordeUne abstention recordRecord abstention

Segundo a RTP e a Lusa, a abstenção ficou próxima de 70%, contra 52% na eleição presidencial de 2021. O país tinha cerca de 419.400 eleitores inscritos, distribuídos por cerca de mil mesas nas ilhas e 240 no exterior.

Selon la RTP et Lusa, l'abstention a avoisiné 70 %, contre 52 % lors de la présidentielle de 2021. Le pays comptait environ 419 400 électeurs inscrits, répartis dans un millier de bureaux sur les îles et 240 à l'étranger.

According to RTP and Lusa, abstention was close to 70%, compared with 52% in the 2021 presidential election. The country had about 419,400 registered voters, spread over some 1,000 polling stations on the islands and 240 abroad.

The diaspora carries weight in Cape Verdean politics: about 76,400 voters, or 18% of the total, are registered abroad, according to Lusa. Portugal and France are among the archipelago's traditional emigration destinations. Neves was prime minister from 2001 to 2016 and has been president since 2021; he studied public administration at the Getulio Vargas Foundation in Brazil.
