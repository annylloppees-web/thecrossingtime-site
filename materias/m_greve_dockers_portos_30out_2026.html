Os estivadores de 24 portos franceses, de Dunkerque a Marselha-Fos e La Réunion, estão em greve de 24 horas nesta sexta-feira, 30 de outubro, por convocação da federação de portos e docas da CGT. Segundo a The Connexion, até 95% da atividade portuária pode parar, e os trabalhadores foram orientados a não carregar nem descarregar navios. As balsas de passageiros não devem ser afetadas, mas o frete sim: profissionais do setor estimam que o acúmulo de contêineres pode levar até quatro dias para ser absorvido, o que pode atrasar encomendas vindas do exterior.Les dockers de 24 ports français, de Dunkerque à Marseille-Fos et La Réunion, sont en grève de 24 heures ce vendredi 30 octobre, à l'appel de la fédération nationale des ports et docks CGT. Selon The Connexion, jusqu'à 95 % de l'activité portuaire pourrait s'arrêter, et les salariés ont reçu la consigne de ne charger ni décharger aucun navire. Les ferries de passagers ne devraient pas être touchés, mais le fret oui : des professionnels estiment que l'engorgement des conteneurs pourrait mettre jusqu'à quatre jours à se résorber, ce qui peut retarder des colis venus de l'étranger.Dockworkers at 24 French ports, from Dunkirk to Marseille-Fos and Réunion, are on a 24-hour strike on Friday 30 October, called by the CGT ports and docks federation. According to The Connexion, up to 95% of port activity could stop, and workers were told not to load or unload any ship. Passenger ferries are not expected to be affected, but freight is: industry professionals estimate the container backlog could take up to four days to clear, which may delay parcels arriving from abroad.

According to Mediaterranee, the CGT says it represents nearly 90% of workers in the sector and reports full participation at Fos; the 95% stoppage figure is a union estimate.

O sindicato diz protestar contra o uso dos portos para transferir material militar, o que chama de "militarização" das instalações, contra o projeto de orçamento de 2027 e contra a automação das tarefas de movimentação de carga. A greve responde a um chamado do Conselho Europeu dos Estivadores e, segundo a The Connexion, ocorre em cerca de 20 países, com o lema "Ports for Peace, Decent Jobs" (portos pela paz, empregos dignos). Sindicatos da Espanha, Portugal, Itália, Alemanha, Grécia e de países nórdicos aderiram, segundo a Mediaterranee.Le syndicat dit protester contre l'utilisation des ports pour transférer du matériel militaire, qu'il qualifie de « militarisation » des installations, contre le projet de budget 2027 et contre l'automatisation de la manutention. La grève répond à un appel du Conseil européen des dockers et, selon The Connexion, touche une vingtaine de pays, sous le slogan « Ports for Peace, Decent Jobs ». Des syndicats d'Espagne, du Portugal, d'Italie, d'Allemagne, de Grèce et des pays nordiques s'y associent, selon Mediaterranee.The union says it is protesting against ports being used to move military equipment, which it calls the "militarisation" of port facilities, against the 2027 draft budget and against the automation of cargo handling. The strike answers a call from the European Dockworkers Council and, according to The Connexion, is taking place in about 20 countries under the slogan "Ports for Peace, Decent Jobs". Unions in Spain, Portugal, Italy, Germany, Greece and the Nordic countries have joined, according to Mediaterranee.

Les liaisons avec la Corse et d'autres destinations méditerranéennes doivent fonctionner, avec de possibles perturbations ponctuelles. L'impact se concentre sur le fret : navires en attente, conteneurs accumulés, frais de stockage pour les entreprises et risque pour les produits périssables.According to The Connexion, DFDS confirmed no scheduled crossings were affected, and Brittany Ferries and Irish Ferries showed no disruption on their live trackers. Links to Corsica and other Mediterranean destinations are due to run, with possible minor disruption. The impact is on freight: ships waiting, containers piling up, storage costs for businesses and risk for perishable goods.
